Permissions checked October 15, 2026. These files are redistributed locally with the app; browsers do not contact Wikimedia to play them. No payment or account is required. Noncommercial use alone is not permission to reuse an arbitrary recording; each recording's stated permission was checked.

The downloaded OGG files are unchanged. At playback, the app blends 1.5 seconds at the loop boundary and adjusts amplitude to a quiet common level, with a peak cap. Playback fades in and out. The in-app credits identify both creators even though their permissions do not require attribution.

Soft ambient tones and breathing chimes are synthesized by the app's own code. They contain no external composition or music sample, and no claim is made about therapeutic frequencies.

The recordings' permissions apply to the recordings. They do not assign a license to the rest of the repository. The app does not use a streaming service, subscription music, CC-NC recording, or a stock library with redistribution restrictions.
